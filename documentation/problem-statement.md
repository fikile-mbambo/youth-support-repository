# Problem Statement

## Background

Young people in South Africa may experience challenges such as mental-health difficulties, gender-based violence, substance-use problems, family difficulties and other social challenges.

Although support services exist, young people may struggle to identify appropriate services, understand what assistance is available, determine where services are located, or know how to contact an organisation.

## Problem

Information about support services can be fragmented across different organisations, websites and community networks.

This creates an information-access barrier for young people who may already be experiencing difficult circumstances.

## Proposed Solution

The Youth Support Directory is a cloud-based platform designed to provide a centralised directory of verified youth-support services.

Users will be able to search for services based on:
Type of support
Location
Age group served
Service availability
Contact method

The platform will provide information such as organisation name, service type, location, operating hours and official contact details.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to explore how cloud computing can be used to improve access to information about youth-support services while applying principles of security, scalability and responsible data management.
