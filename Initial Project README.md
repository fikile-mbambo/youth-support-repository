About the Project
The Youth Support Directory is a cloud-based project designed to improve access to information about youth-support services in South Africa.
The platform aims to help young people identify verified organisations providing services such as mental-health support, counselling, GBV support, substance-use support and other social services.

The Problem
Young people may experience significant social and personal challenges while struggling to identify appropriate support services.
Information about available services can be distributed across different organisations, websites and community networks.
The Youth Support Directory explores how cloud technology can help centralise this information and make it easier to discover.

Proposed Solution
The platform will provide a searchable directory of verified support organisations.

Users will be able to search and filter services according to factors such as:

Province

Location

Type of support

Service availability

Users will be provided with relevant organisation information and official contact details.
